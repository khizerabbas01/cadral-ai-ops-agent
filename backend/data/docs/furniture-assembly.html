Furniture Assembly Service

Booking

Assembly is booked at checkout for £35 per item and takes place on the delivery day by the same two-person team.

Coverage

Assembly is available in mainland England and Wales only. It is not available for orders delivered to Scotland or Northern Ireland.

Self assembly

Every flat-pack item includes illustrated instructions and all fittings. Video guides are available on each product page.
