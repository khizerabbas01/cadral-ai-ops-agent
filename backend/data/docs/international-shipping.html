International Shipping

Where we ship

We ship to all EU countries, Norway, Switzerland, the USA and Canada. Furniture can only be delivered within mainland UK.

Costs and times

EU delivery costs £12.99 and takes 5 to 10 working days. USA and Canada delivery costs £19.99 and takes 7 to 14 working days.

Duties and taxes

EU orders under €150 are shipped with duties prepaid (DDP), so there is nothing extra to pay on delivery. Orders to the USA and Canada may be subject to local import taxes which are paid by the customer.
