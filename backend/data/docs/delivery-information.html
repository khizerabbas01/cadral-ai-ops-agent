Delivery Information

UK delivery options

Standard delivery costs £3.99 and arrives in 3 to 5 working days. Standard delivery is free on orders over £40. Next-day delivery costs £6.99 when ordered before 8pm Sunday to Thursday. Nominated-day delivery costs £5.99.

Scottish Highlands and islands

Deliveries to the Scottish Highlands, Northern Ireland, the Isle of Man and the Channel Islands take 4 to 7 working days. Next-day delivery is not available to these areas.

Furniture delivery

Furniture is delivered by a two-person team to the room of your choice within 7 to 14 working days. The delivery charge is £29 per order. We will text you a 2-hour time slot the day before.

Tracking

You will receive a tracking link by email and SMS when your order is dispatched. Tracking can take up to 24 hours to update.
