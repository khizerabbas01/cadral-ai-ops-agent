Payment Methods

Accepted payments

We accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express, PayPal, Apple Pay, Google Pay and Klarna.

Klarna

Klarna lets you pay in 3 interest-free instalments on orders between £30 and £1,000. Klarna is only available to UK customers.

When you are charged

Your card is authorised when you place the order and charged when the order is dispatched.

Failed payments

If a payment fails, the order is held for 24 hours so you can update your payment details in My Orders.
