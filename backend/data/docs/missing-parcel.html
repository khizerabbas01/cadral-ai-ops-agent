Missing or Late Parcels

Parcel marked delivered but not received

Check with neighbours and any safe place you specified. If it has not turned up after 48 hours, contact us and we will open an investigation with the carrier.

Investigation

Carrier investigations take up to 5 working days. If the parcel cannot be found we will send a replacement or a full refund, whichever you prefer.

Late parcels

If your parcel has not arrived 3 working days after the estimated delivery date, contact us and we will chase it with the carrier.
