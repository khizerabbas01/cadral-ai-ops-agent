Price Match Promise

How it works

If you find the identical item cheaper at a UK retailer within 14 days of your purchase, we will refund the difference.

Exclusions

Price match does not apply to marketplace sellers, clearance items, outlet stores, Black Friday prices or bundle offers.

How to claim

Send us a link to the cheaper item and your order number. We will check and refund the difference to your original payment method.
