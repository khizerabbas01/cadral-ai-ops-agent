Gift Cards

Buying gift cards

Gift cards are available from £10 to £250. E-gift cards are delivered by email within 2 hours.

Validity

Gift cards are valid for 24 months from the date of purchase. They cannot be exchanged for cash or used to buy other gift cards.

Lost gift cards

We can replace a lost e-gift card if you provide the original order number. Physical gift cards cannot be replaced if lost.
